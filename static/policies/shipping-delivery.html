Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: date of publication

Delivery options

Sizeplus Outfit offers free collection from its Wuse 2 boutique by prior confirmation, local delivery within Abuja/FCT and courier delivery to supported destinations across Nigeria.

Indicative delivery prices

Wuse 2, Wuse, Maitama, Central Area: ₦3,000–₦4,000. Garki, Jabi, Utako, Gwarinpa: ₦3,500–₦5,000. Kubwa, Lugbe, Life Camp, Lokogoma: ₦4,500–₦6,500. Other FCT satellite towns: ₦5,500–₦8,500. Lagos, Kaduna, Nasarawa, Niger and nearby destinations: ₦6,000–₦10,000. Other Nigerian states: ₦7,000–₦13,000 or more, subject to quotation.

Final delivery charge

These are indicative ranges, not guaranteed fixed tariffs. Charges depend on precise destination, parcel weight/dimensions, urgency and courier availability. The final fee must be disclosed and accepted before payment; no additional ₦1,000 surcharge should be applied to these revised ranges.

Estimated delivery times

Central Abuja: same day or next day when available; other Abuja districts: same day or next day where feasible; outer Abuja: 1–2 working days; other FCT satellite towns: 1–3 working days; nearby interstate destinations: 2–5 working days; other states: 3–7 working days. Estimates are not guaranteed and must be confirmed for each order.

Processing and delays

Orders are processed after payment and stock confirmation. Public holidays, weather, courier capacity and remote destinations may affect delivery. We will communicate material delays and applicable cancellation or refund options.

Customer details and failed delivery

Customers should provide accurate delivery addresses and a reachable telephone number. Where redelivery is necessary because of incorrect information or unavailability, any extra fee will be disclosed and agreed before proceeding.

Problems with delivery

Please contact us promptly about missing, incorrect or damaged items. Applicable remedies are described in the Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy and Nigerian consumer law.
