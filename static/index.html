About Sizeplus Outfit

Sizeplus Outfit is a women’s fashion boutique in Wuse 2, Abuja, offering polished, comfortable and expressive pieces for women who want confidence, quality and style. Our collections are sourced largely from Turkey and surrounding markets, with an emphasis on flattering fits, versatile wardrobe pieces and attentive customer service.
